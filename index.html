FAQ

Got Questions?

What is Tobeez and how does it work? +
Tobeez is Morocco's first smart distribution network that connects individuals (Beezlinkers) with FMCG brands. You use your smartphone to find sales opportunities in your local area, connect retailers with products they need, and earn money for successful connections — all managed through our app.
Do I need any experience or equipment to start? +
No special experience or equipment needed! All you need is your smartphone, an ambitious mindset, and knowledge of your local area. Tobeez is designed to be accessible to everyone, from students to professionals.
How much can I earn with Tobeez? +
Earnings depend on your activity level, the size of your network, and your zone. Our transparent model means you always know what you'll earn — Madkhoul madmoune! The more retailers you connect and relationships you build, the more you earn.
When will the app be available? +
We're launching soon on both iOS and Android. By joining our waitlist, you'll be among the first notified when we launch in your city. Early members get priority access and exclusive benefits.
Which cities is Tobeez available in? +
We're starting with major Moroccan cities including Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Fès, Tanger, and Agadir, with plans to expand nationally. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know when we reach your city.
I'm a brand — how can Tobeez help my distribution? +
Tobeez gives brands direct reach to the last mile, instant market penetration, real-time data insights, and cost-efficient expansion without expensive sales teams. Contact us to learn more about brand partnerships.
